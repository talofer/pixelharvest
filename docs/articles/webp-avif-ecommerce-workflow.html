Why Standard Downloaders Grab Low-Res Thumbnails

Product galleries hide full-resolution 2x/3x/4K zoom assets inside responsive <picture> elements, srcset descriptors (2400w), lazy-load attributes (data-srcset), and CSS background-image styles.
